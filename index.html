Projects

E-BOM Costing System

A web app that tracks the monthly cost of a PCBA and explains why it moved.

Each PCBA has a bill of materials with its own revision workflow (draft, submit, approve or reject). Monthly cost comes from quantity, component price and exchange rate, and the app breaks every month-over-month change into price, FX, quantity and BOM effects. It has role-based access for admins, analysts and viewers, an audit trail, and flags exceptions. A cost estimator reads the standard BOM Excel format and works out the landed INR cost.
